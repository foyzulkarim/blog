Cold starts across five edge runtimes

14 July 2026

Every vendor benchmark measures the case that flatters them. Here is the same 1.4 MB handler, deployed unchanged, measured from four regions.

The interesting number is not the median. It is p99 on a cold pool after ten minutes of idle, because that is what your users hit on a link click from a LinkedIn post at 2 a.m. local time.

All numbers in milliseconds unless marked. Lower is better; the marked cell is the best in its column.

Lambda@Edge is an outlier by an order of magnitude on cold p99, which is the number that decides whether a reader sees your page or a spinner.

Method

Requests were issued from Dhaka, Singapore, Frankfurt and São Paulo, ten minutes apart, over 72 hours.

for region in dac sin fra gru; do
  hey -n 2000 -c 50 -H "x-probe: cold" \
    "https://$region.probe.example/handler?ts=$(date +%s)" \
    | tee "results/$region-$(date +%Y%m%dT%H%M).txt"
done
